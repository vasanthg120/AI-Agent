A look at your AI assistant

Haive works alongside your team, all day.

It keeps an eye on your customers and your pipeline, answers questions about your own business, and can act on your behalf — drafting a message, chasing a follow-up, or getting your team ready for what's next. Below is what that actually looks like across a working day.

Morning

Starts the day already caught up

Before anyone opens a spreadsheet, Haive can hand your team a short briefing — what needs attention today, and what's already waiting.

A ready-made priority list

Instead of starting the day by hunting through emails and the CRM, your team opens Haive to a short list of what actually needs their attention — overdue follow-ups, upcoming deadlines, quotes still waiting on a decision.

Give me my morning briefing

Nothing forgotten overnight

Enquiries that came in after hours, or customers who haven't heard back in a few days, are surfaced automatically rather than relying on someone remembering to check.

During the day

Keeps the sales pipeline moving

Haive doesn't just report numbers back to you — it looks at your deals the way an experienced sales manager would, and tells you what to do about it.

Spots deals going quiet

It can scan your open opportunities and point out the ones that have stalled, or quotes that have been sitting without a response — the things that are easy to lose track of.

Coaches, not just reports
